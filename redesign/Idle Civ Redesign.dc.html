What changed from 1b
  • POP 3/3 +0.02/s joins the ledger; the red-at-cap value carries the blocker, so the "housing is full" sentence is gone.
  • Your People loses its number pile — tiles, jobs, and one growth line only.
  • Chronicle sheet warms to a pale legal-pad cream with ruled lines and a margin rule; marks are ● ! + ·
  • Card descriptions move to a hover box that also carries the refusal reason ("Short 24 wood.").
